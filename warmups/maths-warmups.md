# Maths Warm-ups

3 minutes, 5 questions. Say the formula before the numbers. Answers are under each set.

## Set A

1. Revenue went from 80M to 68M. What's the % drop?
2. Conversion went from 20% to 25%. Change in pp, and in %?
3. A plan costs 9 a month and has 50k subscribers. Revenue per quarter, and per year?
4. A promo costs 40 per new subscriber, who makes 8 gross profit a month. Months to break even (ignore churn)?
5. Revenue 50M, costs 30M. Revenue +10%, costs +20%. Change in profit?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. 12 ÷ 80 = **15%**
2. **+5pp**, or 5 ÷ 20 = **+25%**
3. 9 × 3 × 50k = **1.35M a quarter**; 9 × 12 × 50k = **5.4M a year**
4. 40 ÷ 8 = **5 months**
5. Change only: revenue +5M, costs +6M → profit **−1M** (−5%)
</details>

## Set B

1. Profit fell 20% while customers grew 10%. What happened to profit per customer?
2. Profit per customer fell from 1.00 to 0.73. What % rise gets it back?
3. A trade earns 0.50 and costs 0.25. A user gets 2 free trades. How many trades a month before they're profitable?
4. 65% of users are on Basic, 35% on Plus. Next month: Basic 65%, Plus 30%, Pro 5%. Per 100 users, how many moved, and from where?
5. Fee 2.0% on 250M; processing cost rises from 0.8% to 0.9% on 300M. How much of the processing cost increase is the rate rise?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. 0.8 ÷ 1.1 ≈ 0.73 → **−27%**
2. 1 ÷ 0.73 ≈ 1.37 → **+37–38%**
3. Profit = 0.5 × (t − 2) − 0.25 × t = 0.25t − 1 → profitable above **4 trades**
4. **5 per 100**, all from Plus (Basic didn't move)
5. Rate rise: 0.1% × 300M = **0.3M**. Volume: 50M × 0.8% = 0.4M. Total 0.7M.
</details>

## Set C

1. 800k customers, cost per customer rises from 8 to 9. Cost increase?
2. A discount of 40% on a 30 plan. What does the customer pay, and what's the discount per customer?
3. 60% of trial users leave when the trial ends. If a trial costs 9 per user, what's the cost per user who stays?
4. Annual target 24.5M with 1.1M customers. Profit per customer needed?
5. Promo customers: 9 profit a quarter, acquisition cost 45. Full-price: 21 a quarter, cost 30. Payback for each (ignore churn)?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Change only: +1 × 800k = **+0.8M**
2. Pays **18**; discount **12**
3. 9 ÷ 0.4 = **22.5**
4. 24.5M ÷ 1.1M ≈ **22**
5. Promo 45 ÷ 9 = **5 quarters**; full-price 30 ÷ 21 ≈ **1.4 quarters**
</details>

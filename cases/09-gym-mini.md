# 09 · Gym Chain (10-minute mini)

**Type:** unfamiliar industry · **Time:** 10 min · **Main skill:** fast frame, goal and tree; check both branches

## Opening prompt (read aloud)

> "A gym chain in Singapore saw monthly profit fall 25%, from S$400k to S$300k, even though members grew 10%, from 20,000 to 22,000. What's going on? Give me your frame, goal and tree out loud before you ask for any data."

<details>
<summary><b>Interviewer only: data key</b> (don't open if you're the candidate)</summary>

- **Profit model:** membership fees are the only revenue (classes included). Costs: staff, rent, utilities, equipment.
- **Plans:** Standard S$100 a month; Premium (24/7 access) S$150 a month.
- **Mix:** last month 16,000 Standard / 4,000 Premium (revenue S$2.2M); this month 19,000 / 3,000 (S$2.35M).
- **Costs:** S$1.8M → S$2.05M. Staff +S$150k (new late-night shifts at all 10 gyms); utilities +S$100k (air-conditioning in a heatwave, seasonal).
- **What changed:** no promotion. Late-night hours were extended at all gyms, **and Standard members get them too**. 2,000 Premium members downgraded to Standard.
- Late-night visits are only 8% of all visits.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Model answer</b></summary>

- **Goal:** back to ~S$400k a month; with 22,000 members that's ~S$18 per member (not S$20). Within two months, since memberships need a month's notice.
- **Breakdown:** Standard +3,000 × S$100 = +S$300k; Premium −1,000 × S$150 = −S$150k; staff −S$150k; utilities −S$100k (seasonal). Total −S$100k ✓.
- **Cause:** extended hours made Premium's 24/7 perk less special (2,000 downgrades) while adding staff cost for just 8% of visits; a heatwave added seasonal utility cost.
- **Fixes:** keep late hours only at the busiest gyms (saves ~S$100k a month); keep late hours as a Premium-only perk to stop downgrades. Trade-off: some Standard members will be unhappy, so test at two gyms first.
- **Common mistakes:** a S$20 per member target (that's S$440k); proposing to "check whether Premium users downgraded" after already being told they did; saying late hours "explain" the cost rise without checking the cost breakdown.
</details>
